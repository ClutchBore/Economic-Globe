Reading it honestly

Four things worth knowing before drawing conclusions from a comparison.

“Latest” is not the same year everywhere

Inflation, exports and internet use span 2016 to 2025. One country's newest reading can be nine years older than its neighbour's, and both appear as current. Each country's panel shows the actual year.

Exchange rates mix two clocks

45 widely-traded currencies carry today's market rate and the day's movement. The rest carry a yearly average, and show no daily change. The US dollar and currencies fixed to it show nothing at all — against itself, the rate is always 1.

Government debt is not comparable

Germany, France, Italy, Japan and most of the euro area are blank — not for want of statistics, but because they report debt on a broader basis than this measure covers. Among the 63 that do report, each counts it differently. Read single countries, not the ranking.

Most countries have no bond yield

A yield only exists where a country issues and trades 10-year debt in its own currency. That is roughly the OECD and few others, so the blanks here reflect the world's bond markets rather than missing records.

Which countries are included

The list follows the World Bank, which counts economies rather than countries — territories that keep their own statistics are counted separately.

  • Hong Kong, Puerto Rico, Greenland and about twenty similar territories appear separately because they publish their own economic statistics.
  • Taiwan is absent entirely — the World Bank publishes no data for it, despite its size.
  • Some small states carry full data but are too small to draw at the map's resolution, so they appear in lists and search rather than on the globe.

How current it stays

Everything is re-fetched from its publisher once a day, so the site follows each institution's own release schedule rather than a snapshot taken at build time.

Nothing is invented to fill a gap

Where a publisher reports no figure, the site shows a blank. Values are never carried forward from an earlier year, estimated from neighbours, or interpolated. A missing number means the number is genuinely missing.
